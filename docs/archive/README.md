rewriting the scientific history.

## Local-only cache

The temporary prior-art downloads formerly under `tmp/pdfs/` were moved to
`.harness/archive/2026-07-15-prior-art-pdfs/`. That directory is intentionally
ignored by Git: the papers are external source material, not project evidence.
The reviewed citations and novelty conclusion belong in the experiment/decision
records, not in committed copies of third-party PDFs.

The manuscript, checked-in evidence, ADRs, R03A implementation, and reusable
analytic comparator are deliberately **not** archived. They remain necessary
for provenance, regression testing, and future matched baselines.
